## 1. Legal Safe Harbor Statement

If you conduct vulnerability research in good faith and in compliance with this policy:
- We consider your activities authorized and will not initiate legal action against you.
- We will work with you to understand and resolve the issue quickly.
- We will publicly acknowledge your responsible disclosure in our **Hall of Fame** (if desired).

---

## 2. In-Scope Assets

- `praebellum.com` and all official subdomains (`*.praebellum.com`)
- Cloudflare Pages edge deployments
- Public APIs and web applications operated directly by Praebellum

### Out of Scope:
- Denial of Service (DoS / DDoS) attacks
- Social engineering (phishing, vishing) against employees or contractors
- Physical security attacks against facilities or data centers
- Third-party vendors or external SaaS providers

---

## 3. Vulnerability Reporting Channels

Please submit all vulnerability reports directly through encrypted channels:

- **Email**: `security@praebellum.com` / `contact@praebellum.com`
- **PGP Encryption**: For sensitive findings, please encrypt your submission using our official PGP key or include your public key for encrypted communications.
- **Subject Format**: `[VULN-REPORT] <Vulnerability Type> in <Target>`

### Response SLA:
- **First Response / Acknowledgment**: Within **24 hours**.
- **Triage & Severity Classification**: Within **72 hours** (CVSS v3.1 / v4.0).
- **Remediation & Patch Deployment**: Prioritized based on severity (Critical: < 48h, High: < 7 days).
